cloud-mask-composite-images

Builds one cloud-masked Sentinel-2 or Landsat image of your area for a chosen period on Google Earth Engine. Clouds and their shadows are masked pixel by pixel, the clear observations are combined into one composite with optional spectral indices such as NDVI, and the result is saved as a GeoTIFF and shown on an interactive map.

What it produces

  • A cloud-masked composite GeoTIFF of the area: the selected reflectance bands, the selected spectral indices, and a valid_count band giving the number of clear observations behind each pixel. 10 m for Sentinel-2, 30 m for Landsat.
  • An interactive map of one band (NDVI by default) or a true/false-colour view, over Esri topographic and satellite base maps, with a legend.
  • A dashboard showing the map, with the workflow name and time range.

How it works

  1. Area. Reads your area of interest from an EarthRanger spatial feature group, keeps its polygons, repairs invalid geometries and merges all features into one shape.
  2. Scenes. Finds the Sentinel-2 or Landsat 5/7/8/9 scenes over the area in the time range, leaving out scenes cloudier than the threshold.
  3. Cloud mask. Masks cloud, cloud shadow and cirrus pixels in each scene using the sensor's quality band.
  4. Composite. Combines the clear pixels over time (median by default) and adds the chosen indices and valid_count.
  5. Export. Downloads the composite tile by tile into one GeoTIFF.
  6. Map. Colours the chosen band and lays it over the base maps on the dashboard.

The Technical Guide covers each stage in detail.

Requirements

  • Access to a Google Earth Engine data source, set up as a connection
  • Access to an EarthRanger data source, set up as a connection
  • An EarthRanger spatial feature group outlining the area of interest, with polygons
